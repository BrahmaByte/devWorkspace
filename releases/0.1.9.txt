DevDashboardV1 0.1.9

Proxy connection diagnostics
- Fixed proxy CONNECT rejection being incorrectly reported as user cancellation.
- Logs and connection errors now include the rejected tunnel's HTTP status, with
  guidance for access policy, sign-in redirects, proxy authentication, endpoint
  configuration, rate limiting and upstream connectivity.
- Provider credential errors remain distinct from proxy access errors.
- Actual caller cancellation and request deadlines are distinguished from
  internal transport aborts, and nested TLS/network failures remain visible.

Logs still omit credentials, URLs, headers, content and raw errors. TLS checks,
no-direct-fallback and no-automatic-retry protections are unchanged.
