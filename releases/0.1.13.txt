DevDashboardV1 0.1.13

Dashboard polish
- The dock launcher closes its empty primary pane before revealing the dashboard
  editor.
- URL-group details no longer repeat the group name; open and delete actions stay
  compact and aligned.

Jira board filters
- Added board search by issue key or summary.
- Added a Jira-style field filter for Parent, Assignee, Status, Work type and
  Labels. Multiple values within a field are combined, while fields combine
  together. These filters refine loaded issues and do not rewrite saved JQL.
- Local-only cards remain local and are filtered only by supported board fields.
- The starter guide now describes the shipped board search and field filters.
