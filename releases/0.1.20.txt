DevDashboardV1 0.1.20

Calendar updates
- Import local Outlook-style JSON events and yearly holidays from files up to
  20 MB, with a 5,000-entry limit per file.
- Filter the Planner by source and entry type. Home shows the next meeting and
  today's schedule from loaded calendar data.
- Remove Confluence Team Calendar controls and loading. Existing subscription
  data is retained locally but no longer used.

Reliability
- Preserve working Jira and Confluence credentials if a connection update cannot
  be saved.
- Reject imported holidays that overlap saved leave or another holiday.
- Load GitHub organization discovery in batches of 500 repositories, with an
  explicit control to continue.
