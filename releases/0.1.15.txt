DevDashboardV1 0.1.15

Calendar and leave safety
- Add or edit plans, holidays and leave from a focused popup opened from Day,
  Week, Month or Year. Failed saves retain the draft.
- Day-based leave is calculated from selected dates, supports one half day, and
  can exclude weekends and known holidays. Leave and holiday ranges cannot
  overlap; plans may share dates.
- Annual allowances are enforced for new leave, edits and allowance changes.
  Existing overbooked records remain visible for safe correction and are clearly
  identified rather than silently changed.
- Connect up to five read-only Confluence Team Calendar iCal subscriptions.
  Subscription URLs stay in VS Code SecretStorage; fetched events remain in the
  current session and holiday calendars block conflicting leave.

Developer workflow
- Connect GitHub.com in Settings, browse authorized account and organization
  repositories, clone with native Git after folder selection and confirmation,
  and add successful clones directly to Project launcher. Personal and
  Enterprise Managed User account names are supported.
- Load accessible Jira saved filters while keeping board field filters separate
  from the saved JQL.
- Preserve supported static Confluence macro formatting while continuing to
  block scripts, active frames and unsafe markup.

Compatibility and data
- Existing calendar entries, leave types, legacy hour units, colors and layouts
  are preserved. Calendar records continue to use the local database and its
  snapshot policy.
- GitHub and Team Calendar credentials or subscription links never enter the
  dashboard Webview or local database.
