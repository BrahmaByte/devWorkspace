DevDashboardV1 0.1.10

Getting started
- The dashboard opens a skippable, page-by-page starter tour on first use.
- Back, Next, Skip and Escape help users explore without changing their data.
- Replay from the header guide icon or DevDashboardV1: Show starter guide.
- Returning users see a short What's new walkthrough once per installed version.
- The last shown version is remembered locally in VS Code global state.

Includes the recent secure proxy diagnostics and accurate CONNECT rejection
messages. Credentials stay in SecretStorage; guides do not execute commands or
create sample data. No Marketplace publication is performed by the local build.
