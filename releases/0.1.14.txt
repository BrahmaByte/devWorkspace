DevDashboardV1 0.1.14

Local calendar planner
- Plan timed agendas in Day, Week and Month views, with a day timeline and
  twelve-month yearly calendar alongside.
- Add inclusive holiday ranges and record leave in days or hours, including
  half days. Set annual leave allowances; saved leave reduces the balance,
  and edits or deletions recalculate it.
- Choose custom colors with native color pickers. Holidays have independent
  colors, gold by default, and diamond markers; leave uses its type color
  and circular markers.
- Aligned calendar card sizes and simplified leave-management forms. Saved
  manual card sizes and order are preserved.

Data safety
- Calendar data stays local and is included in existing database snapshots.
  Migrations preserve saved entries, leave types and legacy units/colors.
- Quantities are recorded as entered; weekends and holidays are not deducted
  automatically. Allowances repeat annually without carryover. Split overnight
  plans and cross-year leave into separate entries.
- The starter guide and update highlights now cover the calendar planner.
